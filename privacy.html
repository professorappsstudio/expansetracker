1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to Expence Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App"). Your privacy and financial sovereignty are of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, storage, and processing of information when you download, install, and use the Expence Tracker mobile application on Android devices.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Expence Tracker is designed as an offline-first financial ledger. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal financial transactions to any remote marketing servers. Your records remain strictly under your control.

2. Information You Provide & Information We Process

To provide personal budgeting and group settlement calculations, the app stores data that you voluntarily create:

  • Transaction Records: Expense amounts, income entries, categories (e.g., Food, Travel, Rent, Bills), notes, timestamps, and chosen currencies.
  • Group & Settlement Data: Group names, custom group descriptions, participant names, and individual share breakdown allocations.
  • User Profile & Preferences: Your chosen display name, preferred currency symbol (e.g., $, €, ₨, £), category customization, and theme preferences.

We do not collect your government ID, credit card numbers, banking login credentials, or location history.

3. Local Storage Architecture

All transaction ledgers, groups, categories, and account summaries are stored locally on your device within the application's sandboxed private storage (via Android Room / SQLite Database).

This data is inaccessible to other third-party applications installed on your Android device due to Android's secure application sandbox architecture.

4. Biometric Authentication & Security

Expence Tracker offers an optional biometric lock feature (Fingerprint / Face Unlock / Device PIN) to protect your financial ledger from unauthorized physical access to your device.

  • The biometric authentication is handled exclusively by the official Android BiometricPrompt API.
  • No Biometric Data is Ever Stored or Seen by the App: The authentication occurs within the Android device's hardware-backed Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) or Secure Enclave. The app only receives a cryptographic boolean result (success or failure).

5. Group Bill Splitting & Shared Data

When you use the group settlement feature:

  • Member names you enter into groups are saved locally on your phone for math and ledger calculations.
  • If you export or share a settlement summary (e.g., via SMS, WhatsApp, or email), that text is processed using standard Android System Share Sheets that you initiate explicitly.

6. Device Permissions Requested

Expence Tracker requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its intended features:

  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Required to enable the optional biometric app lock screen.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional): Required on Android 13+ only if you configure daily expense reminders or settlement alerts.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional): Only requested if you choose to attach receipt photos or export backup files.

You may revoke any optional permission at any time through your Android device settings without losing access to core tracking features.

7. Third-Party Libraries & Services

The application does not embed intrusive advertising networks, third-party user tracking brokers, or spyware. The application operates using standard Android Jetpack libraries and Google Play Services required for application distribution, crash diagnostics, and core runtime operation.

8. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because all your data is stored locally on your device:

  • Permanent Deletion: You can completely and permanently delete all stored data at any time by going to your device's Settings > Apps > Expence Tracker > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Manual Reset: You can also delete individual transactions, categories, or groups directly inside the application.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Expence Tracker does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13. If you become aware that a child has provided personal information without parental consent, please contact us so we can guide you on clearing the local data.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be indicated by the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
